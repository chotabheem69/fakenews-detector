Technical Documentation

How BERT Detects Misinformation

An in-depth look into Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers and fine-tuning on the IEEE DataPort Fake News dataset.

01

WordPiece Tokenization

The headline and body text are split into subword tokens with special delimiter markers [CLS] (Classification Token) at the start and [SEP] separating sentences.

02

Bidirectional Attention

12 Transformer layers with 12 self-attention heads evaluate words in relation to both left and right context simultaneously, capturing subtle deception and incongruence.

03

[CLS] Embedding Pooling

The 768-dimensional hidden state corresponding to the [CLS] token aggregates the semantic representation of the entire news article into a single vector.

04

Classification Head

A linear layer (Linear(768, 2)) followed by Softmax converts the vector into class probabilities for REAL vs FAKE with calibrated confidence scores.

Training on the IEEE DataPort Fake News Dataset

To fine-tune BERT on the official IEEE DataPort Fake News dataset, follow these simple steps:

  1. Download Dataset: Obtain the CSV from IEEE DataPort Fake News Dataset or Kaggle.
  2. Place CSV in project: Save the file as data/fake_news_dataset.csv.
  3. Run the fine-tuning script:
    python train_bert.py --data_path data/fake_news_dataset.csv --epochs 3 --batch_size 16
